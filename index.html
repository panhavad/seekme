  • Move with WASD / arrows, or drag anywhere on touch.
  • Seeker heat: the lantern scorches stone. Stones glow red where the seeker lingered, and fade as they cool.
  • Hider frost: the cold ghost leaves blue footprints that deepen the longer it stands still.
  • Melt ponds: stand still too long as the hider and the frost melts into a permanent puddle.
  • Wall skip: tap Space (or the ⇥ button) to slip through a single wall. It is a slow charge — it winds back up faster while you keep walking, and barely at all while you loiter.
  • Splash! Any ghost stepping in a pond makes a sound both sides can hear and locate.
  • Emotes: tap the pad in the top-right (or press 1–8) to throw a feeling — 😲 😠 😢 😨 😱 😤 😍 😝 — at your friend.
  • Win: hiders survive the clock, seekers catch the hider as fast as possible.
  • Offline: play with no connection; scores queue up and sync to the leaderboard when you reconnect.
  • Online: open the Online tab, create a code and share it — your friend types it in and you play the same maze together.
  • Themes: dress the maze as an old temple, a garden park or a neon city in Settings. Looks only — the maze and the rules never change.
